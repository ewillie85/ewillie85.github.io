01 / ABOUT

Thirty years
with the guitar.

I've been playing guitar for 30 years, following the instrument through different places, sounds, and seasons of life.

I'm a traveler and the owner of a small food forest, where guitar and hand drums have become part of the landscape.

My music lives somewhere between jazz, blues, and folk — often taking the form of short guitar pieces inspired by places, moments, and the feeling of a particular scene.
